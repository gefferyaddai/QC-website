How we invest

The University of Calgary Quantitative Investment Society is a student organization that researches, builds and tests systematic trading strategies: rules-based, data-driven approaches, run the way a quant firm works, at student scale.

  1. Market equity research

    Financial analysts conduct equity research and choose the ones worth covering.

  2. Hypothesize signals

    Quant analysts look for trading signals (patterns that may predict returns).

  3. Test before trading

    Software engineers code each signal and backtest it against benchmarks.

  4. Paper trade with limits

    Approved signals are paper traded with position and daily-loss limits, and results go up on a live dashboard.

What members get

Practical deployment across fundamental, quantitative, and engineering mandates. Members build institutional research workflows, execute data-driven backtests, and produce tangible portfolio work regardless of their academic background.
