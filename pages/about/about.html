Founded at the University of Calgary, The Quantitative Investment Society brings together students interested in quantitative markets and financial engineering. Members collaborate across dedicated tracks in Software Engineers, Financial Analysts, and Quantitative Analysts, developing practical skills through collaborative research, portfolio modeling, and algorithmic development.
