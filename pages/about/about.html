How we work

The University of Calgary Quantitative Investment Society is a student club that researches, builds and tests systematic trading strategies: rules-based, data-driven approaches, run the way a quant fund works, at student scale.

  1. Pick the stocks

    Financial analysts research companies and choose the ones worth covering.

  2. Find the signals

    Quant analysts look for trading signals (patterns that may predict returns).

  3. Test before trusting

    Software engineers code each signal and backtest it against benchmarks.

  4. Paper trade with limits

    Approved signals are paper traded with position and daily-loss limits, and results go up on a live dashboard.
