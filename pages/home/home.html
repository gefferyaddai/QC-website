UC Quants is a multidisciplinary student society at the University of Calgary uniting programmers, financial analysts, and researchers under one roof.

We bridge theory and practice, from Black-Scholes to live backtesting, from probability puzzles to job offers.

See what we do
Software Engineers

Engineer high performance trading infrastructure, scalable simulation platforms, and real-time data pipelines. Software engineers design, optimize, and deploy quantitative models into production using Python, and C++.

Financial Analysts

Conduct market research and financial analysis to identify investment opportunities, industry trends, and macroeconomic developments. Acquire, clean, and organize financial data, evaluate company and sector performance, and support analytical workflows used in investment research and portfolio decision-making.

Quantitative Analysts

Develop and research quantitative financial models, conduct backtesting and data analysis, evaluate market trends and risk factors, and present data-driven investment insights to the organization.
